# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Self-care enthusiasts and individuals preparing for special occasions (weddings, festivals, celebrations). They value natural ingredients, artisanal craftsmanship, and the slow, meditative ritual of henna application. They seek products that feel authentic, safe, and luxurious.

## Product Purpose
To provide a premium, immersive eCommerce experience for handmade henna products. The platform serves as both a high-end boutique and a storytelling medium, connecting the user with the heritage and craftsmanship behind natural henna.

## Brand Personality
Handcrafted luxury meets modern storytelling. The voice is elegant, warm, feminine, and rooted in cultural authenticity. It is refined but organic, sophisticated but grounded.

## Anti-references
- Generic Shopify templates or "AI-slop" visuals.
- Cheap, overcrowded beauty store aesthetics.
- Harsh neon colors or excessive, flashy animations.
- Dark themes (unless used for very specific accents).
- Stock-photo-heavy layouts that feel impersonal.

## Design Principles
- **Rooted in Ritual**: Every interaction should feel intentional and calm, reflecting the slow beauty of henna preparation.
- **Artisanal Precision**: Use fine ornamental details and elegant typography to signal high-end craftsmanship.
- **Calm Editorial Flow**: Prioritize whitespace and luxurious, magazine-style layouts over dense grids.
- **Organic Layering**: Use soft shadows, natural textures, and flowing layouts to create depth and a handcrafted feel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all premium animations.
- Ensure high legibility for serif typography.
- Use earthy, natural tones with sufficient contrast.
